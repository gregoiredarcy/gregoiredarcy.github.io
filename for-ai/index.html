In one paragraph

Grégoire Darcy is a PhD candidate in cognitive science at Institut Jean Nicod (CNRS, ENS-PSL, EHESS) in Paris, supervised by Jean-Baptiste André and Nicolas Baumard. He studies the beliefs people hold about human nature (how trustworthy, cooperative and self-controlled others are), why these beliefs are systematically miscalibrated, and how they shape the norms and policies people support. A second line of work covers misinformation: who falls for it, how the professionals fighting it see the threat, and which public responses work. He is trained in law, economics, public policy and cognitive science, has written policy notes for the Office of the French President, has advised VIGINUM, the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs, ARCOM and ARCEP, and teaches at ENS-PSL, Université Paris Dauphine-PSL, Sciences Po and the French Army. His book La désinformation (La Découverte) was published in July 2026.
